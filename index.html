DENFILUX Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 22, 2026

Welcome to DENFILUX. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide when you visit our website, contact us, or purchase our products.

This Privacy Policy explains how DENFILUX collects, uses, stores, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us, place an order, or interact with DENFILUX, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Delivery address
  • City and state
  • Product, size, and quantity ordered
  • Payment and transaction information
  • Messages or information you voluntarily provide to us

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Process and confirm orders
  • Arrange delivery of your purchases
  • Contact you regarding your order
  • Respond to questions and customer-service requests
  • Provide information about DENFILUX products and promotions
  • Improve our products, services, and customer experience
  • Prevent fraudulent or unauthorized transactions
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

3. Payments

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers. DENFILUX does not intentionally store your complete card or banking credentials when those details are processed directly by a payment provider.

Payment providers may process your information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Delivery Information

When you place an order, we may provide necessary information such as your name, phone number, and delivery address to a delivery or logistics provider so that your order can be delivered.

We only share information that is reasonably necessary to complete the delivery.

5. Marketing Communications

If you choose to receive marketing communications from DENFILUX, we may contact you about new products, promotions, collections, or other brand updates.

You may ask us to stop sending promotional communications at any time by contacting us through the details provided below.

6. Sharing Your Information

DENFILUX does not sell your personal information.

We may share limited information with trusted service providers when necessary to operate our business, including:

  • Payment processors
  • Delivery and logistics providers
  • Website and technology providers
  • Advertising and marketing platforms
  • Professional or legal advisers where necessary
  • Government authorities where required by law

7. TikTok and Advertising

DENFILUX may use advertising platforms such as TikTok to promote our products and reach potential customers.

Where advertising tools collect or process personal information, that processing may also be subject to the relevant platform’s privacy policy and terms.

Where required, DENFILUX will provide appropriate notices and obtain any required consent.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website or advertising platforms may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand how visitors use our services, measure advertising performance, and provide relevant advertising.

You may be able to control cookies through your browser or device settings.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. How Long We Keep Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including fulfilling orders, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, preventing fraud, and meeting legal or regulatory requirements.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of information where legally applicable
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Object to certain processing
  • Request information about how your personal information is being used

To exercise a privacy right, contact DENFILUX using the contact information below.

12. Children’s Privacy

DENFILUX does not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information improperly, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

13. Third-Party Websites

Our website, advertisements, or social-media pages may contain links to third-party websites or services.

DENFILUX is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business, services, technology, or legal requirements.

When we make changes, we will update the Last Updated date at the top of this page.
